Delivery, discount and reward lines are not invoiced alone: Odoo does
not set an order to invoice when only those lines are left. When the
rest of the order has nothing left to invoice, for instance because the
customer finally does not want the goods and their delivery is
cancelled, those lines keep their amount in the sales orders risk of
*Sale Financial Risk* forever.

This module removes the risk of such lines when the other lines of the
order have none. An order with only such lines keeps their risk, as Odoo
invoices them.

When delivery lines are added only once the picking is done, the case
hardly happens and this module is not needed.
