Adds the option "Include all receivable accounts" to credit control
policies. When enabled, the policy searches open items on every account of
type Receivable instead of a fixed account list. This suits charts of
accounts with one debtor account per customer (DATEV style), where new
accounts are created continuously.

   :local:

Usage
=====

Open Invoicing > Configuration > Credit Control > Credit Control Policies,
edit a policy and tick "Include all receivable accounts". The "Accounts"
tab is hidden while the option is active.
