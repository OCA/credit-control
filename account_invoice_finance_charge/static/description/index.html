Finance Charges on Past-Due Invoices

This module adds a review screen that lists every past-due customer invoice that can be charged interest, with a checkbox on each line and Select All and Select None buttons.

The invoices you select are billed as draft customer invoices, one per customer and currency. Nothing is posted or sent automatically.

Configuration

Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Finance Charges and set:

  • Annual rate: simple interest, charged by the day. Check the limits that apply where your customers are located.
  • Grace days: days after the due date before interest starts.
  • Days between charges: how long before an invoice can be charged again.
  • Minimum charge: a lower calculated charge is raised to this amount, unless the open balance is smaller than the minimum.
  • Cap per invoice: most that can be charged on an invoice, as a percentage of its total.
  • Income account and, optionally, Journal.
  • Product, optional, for tax engines that need a product on each line.

Usage

  1. Go to Invoicing > Customers > Finance Charges.
  2. Set the date to charge through, up to today, and click Recalculate if you changed it.
  3. Tick the invoices to charge, or use Select All / Select None. Select All leaves rows in red unticked: those customers have an unapplied payment or credit, or a payment in process, so check them first.
  4. Click Create Draft Charges, then review and post the draft invoices.

Each invoice remembers the period already billed, so the next charge starts where the last one ended. Cancelling or deleting the most recent draft charge makes its period available again. A credit note does not.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Interest is calculated on the balance open today, so a partial payment made during the period lowers the charge for the whole period.
  • Interest starts from the invoice’s final due date, including on invoices with installments.
  • Interest uses a 365-day year.
  • Cancelling an older charge while a newer one exists does not reopen the older period.
  • A credited charge still counts toward the cap per invoice.
  • Each company and branch is set up and run on its own.
  • Invoices with blocked payment are not listed.
  • A draft charge cannot be posted once its source invoice is cancelled or reversed, but a posted charge is not flagged if that happens later.
  • Bank deposits still sitting in suspense are not detected as payments.
