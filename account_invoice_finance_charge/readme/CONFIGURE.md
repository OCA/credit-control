Go to *Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Finance Charges* and set:

- **Annual rate**: simple interest, charged by the day. Check the limits that
  apply where your customers are located.
- **Grace days**: days after the due date before interest starts.
- **Days between charges**: how long before an invoice can be charged again.
- **Minimum charge**: a lower calculated charge is raised to this amount,
  unless the open balance is smaller than the minimum.
- **Cap per invoice**: most that can be charged on an invoice, as a percentage
  of its total.
- **Income account** and, optionally, **Journal**.
- **Product**, optional, for tax engines that need a product on each line.
