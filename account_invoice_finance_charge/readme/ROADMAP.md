- Interest is calculated on the balance open today, so a partial payment made
  during the period lowers the charge for the whole period.
- Interest starts from the invoice's final due date, including on invoices with
  installments.
- Interest uses a 365-day year.
- Cancelling an older charge while a newer one exists does not reopen the older
  period.
- A credited charge still counts toward the cap per invoice.
- Each company and branch is set up and run on its own.
- Invoices with blocked payment are not listed.
- A draft charge cannot be posted once its source invoice is cancelled or
  reversed, but a posted charge is not flagged if that happens later.
- Bank deposits still sitting in suspense are not detected as payments.
