1. Go to **Vendors** and open a vendor form.
2. Navigate to the **Purchase Risk** tab (visible to Purchase Risk Managers).
3. Set the **Purchase Risk Limit** to the maximum exposure allowed for this vendor.
4. Optionally enable **Purchase Risk Exception** to permanently bypass risk checks
   for this vendor.
5. To grant override permissions, add the **Purchase Risk Manager** role to the
   relevant users via **Settings → Users**.
